What is Tensegrity?

Tensegrity (Buckminster Fuller, 1949) = "tensional integrity." Rigid struts float in a continuous network of tension cables — never touching each other.

The structure achieves stability through prestress: cables are always in tension, struts always in compression, and the system reaches force equilibrium at every node.

Applications: geodesic domes, bridges, biological cells (cytoskeleton), robotic arms, space structures.
